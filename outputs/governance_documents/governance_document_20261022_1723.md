## Purpose and Scope
The purpose of this monitoring plan is to establish a framework for the oversight and monitoring of the Credit Decision Support AI system. This plan aims to ensure that the system operates within its intended purpose while minimizing risks to health, safety, and fundamental rights.

## Proposed Content
### Human Oversight Measures [S1]
- Analysts will review outputs from the Credit Decision Support AI and intervene based on predefined error or uncertainty criteria, which are to be established and documented.

### Monitoring Procedures [S3]
- A monitoring schedule will be developed to assess the performance of the Credit Decision Support AI, including metrics for input-data drift, data quality, and analyst overrides.

### Risk Management [S2]
- A risk management system will be implemented to continuously identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks associated with the Credit Decision Support AI throughout its lifecycle.
