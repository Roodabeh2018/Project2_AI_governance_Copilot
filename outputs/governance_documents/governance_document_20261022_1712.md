## Proposed Content
1. **Monitoring Objectives**
   - Ensure effective human oversight of the Credit Decision Support AI to minimize risks to health, safety, and fundamental rights as per [S1].
   
2. **Monitoring Procedures**
   - Analysts will review outputs from the Credit Decision Support AI based on predefined criteria.
   - Proposed criteria for intervention will include error or uncertainty thresholds, which need to be confirmed.
   - The escalation response time will be defined and documented once approved.

3. **Escalation Procedures**
   - A clear procedure for escalating issues identified during monitoring will be established, including criteria for when an analyst must intervene.
   - Proposed escalation response time will be documented once confirmed.

4. **System Restrictions**
   - Procedures for restricting or disabling the system will be developed and documented.

5. **Training and Documentation**
   - Evidence of user training will be collected and retained.
   - Records of analyst overrides will be maintained for accountability.

6. **Review and Reassessment**
   - The effectiveness of oversight measures will be tested at a frequency to be confirmed.
   - A schedule for regular review and reassessment of the monitoring plan will be established.
