# AI Governance Communications Guidelines

Use plain, precise language. Avoid alarmist claims and unsupported legal conclusions. Clearly separate:
- facts supported by a source;
- proposed recommendations;
- unknown information and open questions.

Public LinkedIn-style posts may use only public-tagged source chunks and must not reveal internal system details, risk findings, control weaknesses or confidential implementation status. Use general educational framing and cite public references where available. All generated content requires human review before publication.
