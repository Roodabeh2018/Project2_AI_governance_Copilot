# Northstar Digital Bank — Fictional Company Profile

Northstar Digital Bank is a fictional digital bank created only for this course project. All company and AI-system details are synthetic.

The bank is described in the scenario as a German digital bank exploring AI-assisted processes. Its AI governance functions include Compliance, Operational Risk, Technology Risk, Data Science, Legal and business system owners. The bank wants consistent documentation of AI systems, risks, oversight, monitoring, testing and accountable ownership.

This profile is context for the prototype, not a description of a real institution. Do not make external claims about this fictional bank.
