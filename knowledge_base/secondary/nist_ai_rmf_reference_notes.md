# NIST AI Risk Management Framework — Reference Notes

Official source to obtain: NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) and relevant official companion materials.

The framework organises AI risk management around Govern, Map, Measure and Manage. It is a voluntary framework, not an EU regulation. Use it as a structured risk-management reference, not as a substitute for legal obligations.

These notes are a summary. Verify details against the official NIST publication before making precise claims.
