# EU AI Act — Reference Notes

Official source to obtain and verify: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, available from EUR-Lex.

The applicability and obligations depend on the organisation's role, the system's intended purpose and classification, and applicable dates and transitional provisions. Do not assume that every AI system used by a bank is automatically high-risk or subject to the same obligations.

These notes are only an index to the topic, not the complete authoritative text. Verify exact articles and applicability against the official source before using them in a factual claim.
